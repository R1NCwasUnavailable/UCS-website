What we fix

Laptops and desktops, fixed at component level

The full list from our board — bring the machine in, tell us what it's doing, and we'll tell you what's actually wrong before any work starts.

No power & chip-level repair

Dead board, no power at all, short circuits and liquid damage — traced and repaired at component level instead of just swapping the board.

No display & auto shutdown

Machine powers on but shows nothing, or cuts out mid-use. We find whether it's the panel, the cable, the GPU or the board.

Dull display & white display

Washed-out, dim or fully white screens — backlight, cable and panel faults diagnosed and fixed.

BGA & NVIDIA graphics rework

Graphics chip faults, screen artefacts and dead dGPUs handled with proper BGA rework — not a job every shop can do.

USB port & DC jack

Loose charging ports, jacks that no longer charge, and broken USB sockets re-soldered properly.

Hinges & base moulding

Cracked hinges, lifting lids and broken palm rests rebuilt so the laptop closes and sits properly again.

BIOS password removal

Locked out of a machine you own by a forgotten BIOS or supervisor password — cleared with proof of ownership.

Overall cleaning & servicing

Fan noise, heat and throttling — full strip-down clean, dust removal and fresh thermal paste.

SSD, RAM & spare parts

Chargers, batteries, keyboards, screens, panels, hard disks and RAM in stock — plus NVMe SSD upgrades that make an old laptop feel new.

Desktops & custom builds

Desktop servicing, part sourcing, upgrades and full custom gaming builds assembled and tested in the shop.
